# Parkly research evidence register

Research date: **23 October 2026**, Astana/Almaty time. Only primary sources below support factual statements. Website product claims are evidence of advertised features, not independently tested performance. No interviews, contracts, observed booking results, or market-size survey were available. Pages can change; recheck before a commercial launch.

Research finding: verified alternatives include municipal payment/discovery, venue information, B2B parking software and access-application platforms. This research did **not** verify a citywide Astana marketplace offering guaranteed hourly reservations. This is a research limit, not proof that no competitor exists. Interview operators, test current apps and search local-language app stores before claiming an exclusive market position.
